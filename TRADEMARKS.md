People who see the name should be able to trust that what they run is that toolchain, unmodified. ## You may, without asking

- **Redistribute the unmodified toolchain under the name "Phrase".** Keep the NOTICE file and the licence.
- **Refer to Phrase factually**: "written in Phrase", "compatible with Phrase", "generated by Phrase", "a Phrase-based tool", in articles, talks, tutorials, comparisons, package descriptions and documentation, as long as it does not suggest the official project made or endorsed your work.
- **Link to the project** and show the name in a link or a credit line.
- **Use and ship code the compiler generates for you.** It belongs to you with no obligations from the toolchain (see docs/LICENSING.md).

## You may not

- **Use the name or logo for a modified version.** A fork or a modified build must be renamed and must not use a name or logo that could be confused with the official one. You may say it is "based on Phrase".
- **Suggest the project endorses, sponsors or is affiliated with you** or your product without written permission.
- **Use the name inside the name of a package, product, company or domain** in a way that implies it is the official one (for example an unofficial package published as the official tool).

## Why

The licence lets anyone study, change and reuse the code. The name tells a reader that the toolchain is the official one, with its tests, review record and traceability behind it. A modified version may be better or worse, but the reader should be able to tell the difference.
