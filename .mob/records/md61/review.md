## What held

- **Rotation edge cases:** a launch whose outcome names a different card, context or seat is not served (P1, P1b, P1c). An outcome before its launch does not count (P2). Two launches in one context with one success give the right "last" (P3, P3b, P9). A `fallback` outcome does not count (P5). A launch with no outcome does not count (P8).
- **Old rows:** a legacy row counts exactly as before, even with an outcome line after it (P4, P7). An old-only history stays linear. `rotation_reason` and `mob_process.py record` (`card_seat_log`, `cycles_from_evidence`) accept both shapes (the card's tests pass, and code read).
- **Append-only and read-back:** 12 concurrent `seat_log.py launch` processes and then 12 concurrent `outcome` processes in one git checkout all exit 0, every row parses, and nothing is lost or interleaved (S10, S10b). `seat_log` and `CardRun` share the git-dir lock and the prefix rule.
- **Bad histories:** a non-UTF-8 history or an unparsable line is refused with exit 1 and the file is left byte-identical (S4, S8). Blank lines are tolerated (S7). A newline inside an argument is JSON-escaped and stays one row (S3).
- **Outcome classification:** a reply that merely mentions "402" is not classed as `quota_exhausted` (D6). Timeout and quota failures are classified correctly (the card's tests).
- **Outside a git checkout:** `seat_log.py` warns and still writes (S9).
