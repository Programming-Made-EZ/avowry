# Card 230 retro pick (agile coach seat's voice, proposed by six of seven voices)

PICK: run the logged retro experiment "re-ask only the seats a single condition touches" in the decision windows of the next three cards, as a trial with a stop rule.

The rule for the trial: when a window's round 1 has no No and exactly one seat's condition, the facilitator folds that condition into the text and re-asks only that seat, plus every seat whose cited point the joined text changes; the other seats' NO OBJECTION stands. A full six-seat re-poll stays for any real No, for conditions from two or more seats, and for conditions that conflict. A cite that is not verbatim is still re-asked alone (D171). The facilitator names, in each window record, which seats were re-asked and why the others were not.

Why: on card 230, windows 230-taskforce, 230-slice and 230-scope-2 each ran a full six-seat round 2 for one seat's condition (about 10 to 12 minutes each with Grok disabled); a single condition narrows the text the other seats already accepted. Card 230 is the baseline: five windows, nine rounds of six seats, three single-seat re-asks.

Metric: window wall-clock per card, against card 230's baseline. Counter-metric (Goodhart guard): decisions later reversed, and audit or sign-off findings traced to a re-poll the trial skipped, must not rise; any one such finding ends the trial at once and restores the full re-poll.

Trial scope: the decision windows of the next three cards; then the retro of the third card decides keep or revert. Owner: agile coach.
