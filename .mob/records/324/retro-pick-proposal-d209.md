PICK, one patch with two parts:
P1. A new Experimental entry, "A gate starts on a fresh binary and a clone": after a cherry-pick or merge that changes src/, the facilitator builds the CLI project before the baseline or final gate (the Acceptance suite refuses a stale CLI by design); a full gate or sign-off run of a check that needs history is made in a single-branch clone of main, not a git archive copy; a gate or sign-off brief names both steps and says when a SHIP rests on facilitator-run evidence because the reviewer could not run dotnet.
P2. The D202 byte-parity check moves from the Experimental section into the permanent Engineering discipline practices: the trial of the next three mechanical-rewrite cards (197, 266, 324) found no byte-level defect after a clean report, where card 196 had three. The text is unchanged.
Not changed: every other value, principle, practice, standing rule and Experimental entry (reviewed at this retro: kept, no change). The seat-spread ruling is the product owner's and is not edited; the arithmetic below goes to the product owner in the report.

EVIDENCE (card 324): the first baseline gate failed 63 Acceptance tests because the CLI binary was stale after the facilitator's cherry-pick (the freshness guard said "run dotnet build src/Avowry.Cli"; about 10 minutes lost); the sign-off's gate in a git archive copy showed four failures against the facilitator's 323 of 323 (the three holdout tests need .git, plus one 20 second timing test that passed alone); six of seven voices named the rebuild or the clone or both. Four of seven voices said to adopt the byte-parity check and the others kept it on trial without objection; the facilitator's recompute of the green's bytes matched exactly on both greens of card 324, and no byte-level defect was found after a clean report on cards 197 and 266.

Seat mix (reported, not a document change): Grok carried 3 of the 5 writing seats (60 percent) while Copilot and agy were out of quota. With five writing seats and two drivers a perfect split is impossible, so the ruling's "no more than half on one driver" cannot hold on an odd count; the next card's plan gives Codex the extra seat and the report to the product owner asks whether the ruling means no more than ceil(n/2) per driver per card, or half over a window of cards.

HYPOTHESIS P1: starting the gate on a fresh binary and a clone removes false gate failures that cost a rerun. METRIC: gate or sign-off runs failed by a stale binary or a missing .git, per gated card that changes src (card 324: 2 of 3 runs), target 0. COUNTER-METRIC: added gate minutes (a CLI build is about 3 seconds; a clone adds minutes) and any failure a clone hides. TRIAL: the next 3 gated cards that change src.

User benefit: the ship verdict matches the tree that ships, so a card is not held or rerun for a stale binary, and a safeguard that held on all three trial cards is permanent, which keeps the board moving for Ari and the other consumers.
