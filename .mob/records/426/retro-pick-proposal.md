THE PROPOSED ENTRY (verbatim from the patch):

- **A change to a function that removes, rewrites or recognises text is checked against its old version on generated inputs (experiment; card 426 retro, Decider D256).** While this entry is on trial: when a card changes a function that removes, rewrites or recognises text (a path scrub, a parser, a redaction), the slice names a differential check: the old and the new function run side by side on generated inputs (a committed generator with a fixed seed, at least 100,000 strings built from the shapes the card names, plus the old function's known outputs). Any input where the new output still holds a planted account-like token that the old output did not hold, or where a second pass changes the output, is either a failing test written at the red or a named, accepted difference in the red record; the generator is committed as an offline test that runs in the normal gate, with a failing count pinned and never hidden. The second review keeps its own differential run: it does not rely on this one.
  - Hypothesis: a differential check of old against new on generated inputs, written at the red, catches the regression a card's own change exposes before the second review. Metric: leaks or regressions caused by the card's own change and first found at the second review on such cards (card 426: 1, a path after a JSON escape that the old over-scrub had hidden), target 0. Counter-metric: minutes added to the slice and the red (more than 10 minutes on a card is a miss) and differences that turn out to be benign. Trial: the next 3 cards that change such a function, counted from Decider D256. Owner: the facilitator. Review at the third such card's retro: keep, change or remove.
