STANDING-RULES PREFLIGHT (the facilitator's discipline since the card 176 retro): the Decider protocol steps 2 to 4 (a vote of Yes, Support-it or No; each No names ONE fixed condition; an adopted condition goes back to the same outlier to confirm; a seat given its condition that then raises a new demand breaks its promise; unresolved means the proposal dies): unchanged and used as is. The decision-record rules (step 5, areas.json, the index check): unchanged. No rule contradicts a line of this proposal.

PICK: a new Experimental entry, "The confirmation step replaces the separate independent re-run". While it is on trial, a Decider is recorded after its primary window and, when there was an amendment, the original protocol's confirmation step: each outlier's role is asked, with its own reply quoted, whether its adopted condition is met, and every other voter answers only whether the amendment changes their vote. The separate window of fresh seats on the final text is not run; it is run when any voter or the product owner asks for one, or when the primary window passed with no amendment and no seat voted No (so there is nothing to confirm) and the proposal is a change to this agreement. The record shows the primary window, each amendment and each confirmation, and carries one committed trigger line that an auditor can `git show`, naming which trigger applied: (a) the confirmation step completed, so the re-run was not run; (b) the re-run was run because a named voter or the product owner asked; (c) the re-run was run because the primary window passed with no No and no amendment and the proposal changes this agreement; (d) the re-run was not run because the primary window passed with no No and no amendment and the proposal does not change this agreement. The patch is .mob/records/178/agreement-amendment.diff (candidate text agreement-candidate.md).
Not changed: every other value, principle, practice, standing rule and Experimental entry. The two other retro improvements need no agreement change and are applied as lines of the briefs the facilitator writes: the QA brief says to run a characterisation on a tree that already has the new state before the red is filed; the slice brief says to name every file a new process-start wrapper touches (the process-start callers guard list).

EVIDENCE (card 178 and before): the independent re-run raised demands that the primary window's seats never made, on five Deciders (D208, D209, D210, D212, D214: each time a fresh seat in the re-run voted No on a new point while the outliers' own conditions had been met or adopted); the original-protocol confirmation step used from D214 settled D214, D215 (the architect's objection surfaced as a conflict between two outliers' conditions, not as a re-run surprise) and D216 in one window plus one confirmation step each; the declaration on card 178 took about 40 minutes including the Ready draft (card 176: about 100 for three Deciders), and the process-against-build ratio fell from about 4 to 1 to about 1.5 to 1; five of the seven retro voices (the product manager, architect, QA, developer and decider) said to rely on the primary window plus the confirmation and to drop the extra re-run, the coach to keep the parallel window plus the confirmation step "not a full re-run", and the audit lens to keep a re-run "only when the primary run is split".

AMENDMENT AFTER THE RE-RUN OF D217 (the outlier's condition, adopted): the primary window passed with six Yes and no amendment; the fresh-seat re-run (required by this pick's own rule (c), the proposal changing this agreement) had five Yes and one No, from the audit lens: the skip has no detector (an ask is not in git) and the counter-metric cannot be scored without a re-run, so each trial Decider record must carry one committed trigger line naming which trigger applied (adopted above). The audit lens is asked only whether its condition is now met; the other five voters of the primary window are asked only whether the amendment changes their vote.

HYPOTHESIS: the confirmation step gives the check the re-run was meant to give (a result that does not rest on one noisy window) without fresh seats that never made the outlier's promise and raise new demands. METRIC: minutes from proposal to recorded result per Decider (card 176: about 100 for three declaration Deciders; card 178: about 13 with one window and one confirmation step), target at most 20; Deciders that fail on a demand made only by a fresh re-run seat (D208, D209, D210, D212 and D214 had one), target 0. COUNTER-METRIC: single-window errors found after recording (a miscounted vote, a misread cite, a proposal adopted that a re-run would have failed). TRIAL: the next 5 Deciders. Owner: the facilitator.

User benefit: a card reaches its first failing test in minutes, so Dana, Ari and Morgan get working docs, packages and compilers sooner, while the outliers and every other voter still stand behind each result.
