## Criterion 2 against D163 (added at the coordinator's request)

How far it departs. D163 wants balanced rotation: seat the least-recently-used available model first, Claude taking its fair share, and stop only the drift to all-Claude while others have capacity. As built, criterion 2 is a binary, per-seat prohibition: a Claude seat is refused whenever any one other enabled model's probe succeeded (quota_first_problem, mob_process.py; gate_seat, facilitator.py). That is the "no Claude rule per se" the product owner did not ask for. Consequences: (a) no rotation exists at all, so the check never says which model to use, only that Claude may not be it; (b) it forbids legitimate Claude shares (a mixed roster with Claude doing one of five seats is refused while any model has quota), which is why TrueMobbing had to be rewritten (F2); (c) it uses no history, so it cannot tell "Claude took its share" from "everything went to Claude"; (d) it blocks a roster that the declared task force fixed in advance rather than steering the pick. Departure is large for the rule, small for the plumbing.

What should survive the rework.
- Probe freshness (Criterion 1: gate_seat re-probe at 60 minutes or more, refuse exit 2 when it cannot probe or keep the evidence, probe adopted only after probes.json is written, taken_at cited in the MOB.md entry and events line). Keep whole: any rotation that picks "an available model" needs a fresh, fail-closed availability read, and it is independent of the rule.
- The probe results shape and fail-closed reading in quota_first_problem's input handling (a missing or ok-less result is "unread", never "no quota"; a disabled backend counts as no quota). Keep as the availability function the rotation calls.
- The pinned probe model (Criterion 3) and its start-time refusal. Keep, with F1 and F3 closed.
- The refusal naming (role, context, models with quota). Keep the mechanism and reword: the refusal should now fire only on the new failure mode (an all-Claude roster, or a Claude share above the ratio, while others are available) and name the least-recently-used available model as the seat to use instead.
- The quota_reason field: keep the field and the rule that it is built from the probe, never typed by a seat, and the writing path in write_record (it survives later runs). Change its content: record the probe taken_at, each model's availability evidence, and the rotation input (the model's last-used seat or count) that justified the pick. Card 210's check can still read the field.
- Replace: the per-seat blanket refusal in gate_seat and the TrueMobbing rewrite (restore a Claude member there once the ratio rule allows it). Needs a new persistent input: per-model last-used or seat counts across the card (and likely across cards), which the current code does not keep.
- Risk to carry into the Decider: any "least-recently-used" metric should be guarded against Goodhart by being computed from the events lines, not from a counter a seat can write.
