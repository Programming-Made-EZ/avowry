PICK, one patch:
P1. A new Experimental entry, "Red before the atomic green": on a card whose green is one atomic change (a rename, a move, a bulk rewrite) the slice states for every criterion whether it is new-state or characterisation, and the card's done checks say so up front; every new-state criterion's test is committed and watched failing against the pre-change tree before the green, one red commit per criterion with its own "Criterion: n" line (a red commit may hold several criteria's tests if the slice says so; the green may carry "Criterion: 1-n"); the red record quotes the failing run; a characterisation criterion's test is committed before the green, passes on the pre-change tree, and is named as characterisation with its reason (it does not count as a red); the task-force proposal names each criterion's red before the Decider votes; the pre-ship gate reruns the criteria's tests against the pre-change tree and every new-state test fails there; the criterion order is checked from git log; mutation evidence stays an extra check and never substitutes for a watched red.
P2. The D196 cite check (trial entry) is adopted into the permanent text: its normalisation of case, markdown emphasis and trailing punctuation, and its echo rule, join the permanent "Cite-a-point votes" practice; the trial entry is removed.
Not changed: every other value, principle, practice, standing rule and Experimental entry (reviewed at this retro: kept, no change; D202's two entries and D157, D175, D158, D154, D190, D166 to D168 are extended one card; D193's G2 was already replaced by the ruling).

METRIC: born-green new-state tests per atomic card (card 197: 8 of 12), target 0; share of criteria whose red commit precedes the green in git log, target 100%.
