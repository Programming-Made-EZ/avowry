What a fresh facilitator reads and writes at a restart; its seat brief is briefs/facilitator-restart.md.

- Read, in order: the wiki index, the "Restart here" section of HANDOFF.md, the brief, the newest re-rank note under .mob/records/reranks/, and the card record.
- Write one report with the fields next_card, next_step, boundary_hash, rules_read and subagent_tokens. The facilitator committed its own key for the first four before the run, and the report is committed unedited after it.
- Do not run commands that change the repository, and do not read other files before the report is written.
