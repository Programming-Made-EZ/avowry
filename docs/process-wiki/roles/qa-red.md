What the QA seat reads and writes at the red step.

- Rule: The red seat writes the intent line first. Quote: "Before the first red of a criterion that implements a finding, MD item or owner direction, the red seat writes". See [D165](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d165).
- Rule: The red body answers the hostile-input list and assumption lines. Quote: "The architect writes it; the reviewer checks the probe at review.". Quote: "the red seat answers each entry in the red commit body". See [D166](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d166) and [D167](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d167).
