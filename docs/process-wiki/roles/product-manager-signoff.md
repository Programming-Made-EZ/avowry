What the product manager reads and writes at sign-off.

- Rule: Sign-off ties each Done check to evidence from git, and metrics are evidence, not targets. See [D154](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d154).
- Rule: The keep, revise or drop verdict of an experiment is decided by Decider at its trial retro. See [D157](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d157).
- Rule: Product work resumes only after the process is locked down in tooling and saved to the template. See [D161](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d161).
