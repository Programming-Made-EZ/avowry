What the technical writer reads and writes for wiki pages, briefs and card text.

- Rule: Reports and pages describe what a card, decision or finding does; an ID may follow in parentheses. See [D161](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d161).
- Rule: A change to the checker needs a decision number whose record is committed first. See [D145](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d145).
- Rule: The wiki exists so that each seat reads the index and only the pages its role and step need. See [D170](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d170).
