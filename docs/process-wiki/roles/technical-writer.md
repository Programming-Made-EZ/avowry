What the technical writer reads and writes for wiki pages, briefs and card text.

- Rule: Reports and pages describe what a card, decision or finding does; an ID may follow in parentheses. Quote: "in anything meant for the product owner or the person running the mob, cards, decisions and findings are described by what they are or do". See [D161](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d161).
- Rule: A change to the checker needs a decision number whose record is committed first. Quote: "scripts/check-*.py, .githooks/ or .github/workflows/ needs in its subject a D-number whose record is in a strict ancestor commit". See [D145](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d145).
- Rule: The wiki exists so that each seat reads the index and only the pages its role and step need. Quote: "Every turn re-reads the whole context, so fresh seats overpay by reading everything and the long facilitator session overpays on every turn". See [D170](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d170).
