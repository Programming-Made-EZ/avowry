Stand-downs: a micro-retro at each cycle boundary with one adjustment, reviewed at the next.

- Rule: A stand-down at each cycle boundary within a card records what worked, what did not and one adjustment. Quote: "Stand-down at each cycle boundary within a card: KEEP AS EXPERIMENT". See [D151](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d151).
- Rule: Every stand-down and retro opens by reviewing each previous adjustment before any new one. Quote: "every stand-down and every retro opens by reviewing each adjustment the team made at the previous stand-down or retro, before any new adjustment". See [D155](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d155).
