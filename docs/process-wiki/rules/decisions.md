Decisions: routine per-card calls take an objection window, rule changes take a full Decider.

- Rule: Routine per-card decisions take an objection window instead of a full Decider. See [D153](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d153).
- Rule: A full Decider is needed on an objection, for a one-way door, or when a standing rule changes. See [D153](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d153).
- Rule: A product owner ruling is recorded as a decision, not voted, and can waive stop-the-line for one card. See [D170](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d170).
