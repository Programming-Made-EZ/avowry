Thin slices: numeric limits per card and commit, and the slice size cap.

- Rule: A card has at most three acceptance criteria and one concern; commits stay within the file and line limits. Quote: "at most three acceptance criteria (scored from the issue body), at most three increments and one concern". See [D136](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d136).
- Rule: The flag-scoring clause of the thin-slice rule was amended on re-vote. Quote: "passed with the flag-scoring clause amended". See [D142](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d142).
- Rule: A larger card is split into numbered slices, each under 15360 bytes, and a criterion is never cut in two. Quote: "A card whose slice would be larger is split into numbered slices (slice 1 of n), each its own commit, each carrying whole criteria; a criterion is never cut in two.". See [D168](../../decisions/process-and-staffing.md#d168).
