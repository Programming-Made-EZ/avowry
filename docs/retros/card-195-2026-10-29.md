## Process improvement pick (one)

- Pick: the slicing rule of D136: the architect decides SHIP AS IS or SPLIT with a rubric (one concern, at most three increments, at most 15 countable files and 400 changed lines, a green gate reachable in one dev turn), and the PM rejects oversized cards at ready time.
- Benefit: commit-to-live per card falls because each card is smaller and needs fewer gate waits.
- Hypothesis: with the rule, the median commit-to-live falls to 45 minutes or less and the 90th percentile to 90 minutes or less.
- Metric: median and 90th percentile of the green CI completion minus started_at over the last five cards; counter-metrics: review-fix and CI-fix commits per card and acceptance coverage; never split a card only to hit the number.
- Automated check: to be built (a card after this one): the slice field, the file and line counts per commit and the exception kinds of D136.
