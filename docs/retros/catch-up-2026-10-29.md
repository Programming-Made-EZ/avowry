## The process lapse (what happened, plainly)

The next card (073) was done by a rotation author in a clone and imported by the facilitator, and that pattern became the default with no decision (D134 records that no decision retired card mode).
- Consequences: no role rotation recorded, `MOB.md` unchanged for three days (it said Usable v1 0 of 6), no retro per Done card, the driver ledger not updated since 2026-10-25, and CI red for 28 hours unseen: it was found by looking at the Actions page on the morning of 10-28, the fix came at 08:20 (46fdfef), and the stop-the-line check (0c01496, 10-28 08:35) was written afterwards. ## Process improvement pick (one)

- Pick: the accountability detector of D134, run outside the facilitator (CI on every push, a 30-minute schedule, a pre-push hook, an external auditor).
- Benefit: the product owner and anyone else can see on GitHub when the process is skipped.
- Hypothesis: moving detection out of the facilitator cuts the unseen-red time and the share of Done cards without a retro or trail to near zero.
- Metric: longest continuous red on main (target under 2 hours); share of Done cards with a trail record and a retro (target 100 percent); the age of `MOB.md` at each push (at most 3 non-MOB commits). Counter-metric against gaming: a retro that is an empty stub, or a trail with one commit reused for every seat, fails the check by design.
- Automated check: `scripts/mob_process.py check`, required.
