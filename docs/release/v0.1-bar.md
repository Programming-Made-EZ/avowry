# v0.1 public preview: the release bar

Status: revised after the Decider (D121), 2026-10-29. Modelled on the Usable v1 bars (`board/MILESTONE-usable-v1.md`): observable bars, each verified by a test or a recorded run, and a gap table.

## Who it is for and what must be true

**Persona: Dana**, a .NET developer who directs an AI agent and is curious about AI-accountable code (`PERSONAS.md`). The preview is releasable when Dana can install Avowry, follow a getting-started guide, write a small spec-first API, and see three things pay off: **verifiability** (specs become running tests), **preserved intent** (the Avowry source states what each avow must do), and **reviewability** (a change can be checked against the spec and traced to it).

The preview is not about beating plain C#. The head-to-head benchmark stays published as context with its limits stated (`docs/results/`). Principle: release the smallest thing that is honestly useful and honestly described, so early readers learn from it and the claims stay inside the evidence.

## Estimate

Sizes: S about 1 to 2 hours, M about 4 to 8 hours, L about 1 to 2 days of the mob's wall-clock time including review rounds and gates.

**Effort:** 14 S and 5 M give roughly 40 to 70 effort-hours before review waits and date gates.

**Wall-clock:** cards closed per day, by the dates on the board statuses (UTC): 2 (25 Oct), 13 (26 Oct), 36 (27 Oct), 19 (28 Oct), 4 so far on 29 Oct. The early days were mostly small documentation and tooling cards. The compiler, emitter and CLI cards of the last day closed at 3 to 6 per day, including stop-the-line fixes, because each takes an author round, a gate of 10 to 11 minutes, a review and a CI run. At 3 to 6 cards a day, 19 required cards take **about 4 to 8 working days of continuous running**, a target that holds only with parallel work (packaging and the guide run beside the compiler cards) and assumes the product owner answers the licence and package-name questions within a day. The Copilot rerun (144) is optional, so its quota date (2026-11-01) does not block.

**Biggest uncertainty:** bar 5, the first measurement. Cards 149 to 151 are first-time designs; sizing could grow from M to L, and review churn on measurement design is likely. The preview publishes whatever the measurement shows, with limits. The second uncertainty is bar 4's actionable diagnostics, whose size depends on how many failure paths the guide has.

## One-way doors (the product owner decides)

- **The licence.** The repository is public with no licence today, which means all rights reserved. Choosing one cannot be undone for versions already published under it.
- **The public package name and its owner** on the package feed, and **publishing the first version**: a published version can be unlisted but not removed.
- **The first public claims** (the README text and the results page): once read, they cannot be recalled; they should state evidence and limits only.
- **The name "Avowry"** (trademark and package-ID collisions), **dependency licence compatibility**, **publishing benchmark outputs from vendor models** (their terms) and **the repository history** (author metadata and a secrets scan): checked in card 186 before anything is published.
- **A 0.1.0 stability expectation**: the preview says the grammar may change before 1.0.

Everything else is reversible: each card is small enough to revert, per the working agreement.

## Order (smallest honest path)

Fit around the running queue (173, then 174 and 175): packaging and the guide are independent of the compiler cards and can run in parallel with them; the evidence cards come after 174 so they run against the corpus; the licence question goes to the product owner now because it blocks nothing until the publish step.
