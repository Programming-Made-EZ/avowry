# Licensing

One page on what you may do with Avowry and with the code it generates.

## The toolchain: Apache-2.0

The compiler, the command line tool, the specifications and the tests in this repository are licensed under the **Apache License, Version 2.0** (the `LICENSE` file). You may use, modify and redistribute them, including commercially. Keep the license and the `NOTICE` file with any redistribution and follow the license text for the other conditions, such as stating that you changed a file. The license includes a patent grant from the contributors for their contributions.

Why Apache-2.0: it is permissive and widely accepted at work, it gives users an explicit patent grant, and its `NOTICE` mechanism keeps attribution without restricting use.

## The name and logo: a trademark policy

The license does not grant the name or logo. Unmodified redistribution may use the name, factual references such as "compatible with Avowry" are fine, and forks and modified versions must be renamed. The short policy is in `TRADEMARKS.md`. The rationale: the name signals the official, accountable toolchain.

## Code the compiler generates: it belongs to you

**Code generated by the Avowry compiler belongs to you, with no license obligations from the toolchain.** This covers everything the compiler generates from your `.avowry` files. You may license, sell, modify or keep it private as you wish, and you do not need to keep a notice, credit Avowry or publish anything. The generated runtime helper files the compiler writes into your project are part of that output and are yours under the same terms.

This applies to what the compiler generates for you. The compiler itself, its source and its documentation stay under Apache-2.0 as above, and examples copied from this repository's `demo/` or `docs/` folders carry the repository license.

## Contributions

Contributions are accepted under the same Apache-2.0 license. A contributing note (card 185) will describe how.

## Status

The copyright holder named in `LICENSE`-adjacent files (`NOTICE`, `TRADEMARKS.md`) is a marked placeholder until the product owner confirms it at the pre-publication checks (card 186). Nothing is published as a release until those checks pass and the product owner approves in writing.
